## The subject of this material

A Franka Panda stands on a pedestal with a flat bat welded to its flange. A pitching machine 6.5 m away throws baseballs at 9 to 13 m/s, with random height, aim and spin. A camera reports the ball's position 100 times a second, 30 ms late, with noise that grows with range. The arm must swing so that the bat meets the ball and sends it to a target zone on the ground several metres out.

The swing is built by an expensive planner: it forecasts where the ball will cross the hitting plane, solves backwards through the impact for the bat pose and speed that send the ball to the target, and solves a trajectory problem for seven joints. Called at every step it costs 48 calls per pitch. The question this project asks is the one in its name: **when is it worth thinking again?**

## The result in one paragraph

A closed-form copy of the simulator's flight step lets an EKF forecast where the ball will cross the plate; that forecast is 17 cm off half a second out and 0.5 cm off in the last 25 ms, so the swing has to be re-planned as the pitch comes in. Re-planned at every step, the analytic planner hits every pitch and lands 97.8 ± 0.2 % of them in the target zone; planned once, it hits 11.8 %. A learned world-model planner trained on 48,000 pitches hits 96.9 % but lands only 54.9 % on target. Among the compute triggers, the classical event triggers (innovation, variance, drift) all lose to a plain fixed rate at the same budget, because they spend their calls early, when the estimate moves by centimetres, and go quiet late, when millimetres decide the hit. A PPO trigger learns the opposite schedule: at about 4 planner calls per pitch it keeps a hit quality of 0.94 against 0.91 for a fixed rate, and at about 3 calls 0.81 against 0.58. Under mid-flight gusts the data-driven triggers spend extra calls and a fixed schedule does not; no trigger recovers the quality a gust costs. As a stretch, two arms with the same stack keep a ping-pong rally going for 10.0 returns on average (11.5 with the true ball state), once they stop replanning while the filter is unsure. All figures are means over three seeds and are regenerated by the scripts in [Running it](/reference/running-it/).
