Every statement in this site that can be checked by a computer in a few seconds is a test. They run with

```bash
env -u PYTHONPATH PYTEST_DISABLE_PLUGIN_AUTOLOAD=1 .venv/bin/python -m pytest tests -q
```

and take about a minute and a half, most of it in simulated presses.

## The bench (`tests/test_bench.py`)

| test | what it establishes |
|---|---|
| `test_home_pose_points_down` | at the home configuration the tool axis is vertical and the tip frame equals $R(\pi)$ of [Action Primitives](/bench/primitives/) |
| `test_ik_reaches_whole_workspace` | 200 random targets over the workspace and all yaws: position error below $10^{-6}$ m, every joint at least 0.05 rad inside its range |
| `test_state_layout_matches_mujoco` | the offsets of `qpos`, `qvel`, `act`, `plastic` and `ctrl` inside the state vector are the ones MuJoCo uses, and there are 6 plastic numbers per cage cell |
| `test_tool_models_share_one_state_layout` | the press, roller and stamp models have identical state layouts |
| `test_vertices_are_trilinear_in_the_nodes` | $V = WN$ agrees with `mjData.flexvert_xpos` to $10^{-12}$ m after a press, and the map from nodes back to `qpos` is its inverse |
| `test_heightmap_exact_on_a_sheared_plane` | the fixed-point height map recovers a tilted plane to $10^{-9}$ m under an arbitrary sideways shear |
| `test_batch_equals_single_thread_bit_for_bit` | a rollout through `mujoco.batch` equals plain `mj_step` on one `MjData`, exactly |
| `test_save_restore_replays_bit_for_bit` | restoring a saved state after unrelated work and replaying an action reproduces the result exactly |
| `test_restore_without_plastic_state_diverges` | the same replay without the plastic strain differs by more than a millimetre |
| `test_dent_is_permanent` | a press leaves a dent deeper than 5 mm that moves by less than 0.05 mm in the following second |

## The prior (`tests/test_prior.py`)

| test | what it establishes |
|---|---|
| `test_envelope_matches_the_simulated_tool` (3 tools × 3 yaws) | the envelope formula equals the tool that MuJoCo simulates: rays cast at the tool head from 400 table points agree with the formula to 0.3 mm on the flat part and 0.2 mm on average, and the footprints agree on 97 % of the points |
| `test_roller_envelope_is_the_union_over_the_travel` | for the roller, the formula with the swept footprint equals the lowest ray hit over 71 positions along the travel |

## The planner (`tests/test_mpc.py`)

| test | what it establishes |
|---|---|
| `test_minjerk_endpoints_and_symmetry` | the time law ends at 1 and satisfies $s(\tau) + s(1 - \tau) = 1$ |
| `test_yaw_wraps_instead_of_clipping` | the yaw range of each tool equals its symmetry period, and a yaw just outside the range reappears just inside the other end |
| `test_circular_mean_across_the_wrap` | yaws clustered at $\pm 45°$ average to $\pm 45°$, not to $0°$ |
| `test_travel_direction_is_the_tool_y_axis` | the travel direction used by the primitives is the second column of the tip rotation |
| `test_cem_finds_a_known_optimum` | on a quadratic cost that is periodic in the yaw, the search finds the optimum and returns candidates sorted by cost |
| `test_guided_proposal_goes_where_the_clay_is_too_high` | proposals centre on the region of excess height and choose the depth that puts the tool bottom at the target height |

## The models (`tests/test_models.py`)

| test | what it establishes |
|---|---|
| `test_shapes_and_pinned_nodes` (4 models) | every model returns a state of the shape it was given, converts to a $32 \times 32$ map, and never moves a pinned node |
| `test_prior_never_raises_and_only_acts_under_the_tool` | the prior lowers vertices under the tool, raises none, moves nothing sideways and changes nothing far from the tool |
| `test_prior_beats_no_change_on_a_real_press` | on one simulated press the prior's height map is closer to the truth than the untouched block |
| `test_graph_has_the_grid_edges` | the graph has exactly the 3,510 directed face-neighbour edges of a $13 \times 13 \times 4$ lattice |

## What is not a test

The numbers in the result tables are measurements, not assertions: prediction errors, planning outcomes, the gains of the fallback. They are regenerated by the milestone scripts ([Running It](/reference/running-it/)) and written to `results/*.json`; the figures and the `results/Mx.md` reports are produced from those files, never typed by hand.
